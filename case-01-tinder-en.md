> Case study. Completed as a practical assignment for the GoPractice "Product Manager" program. Product: Tinder; the work is not affiliated with the company.

## Situation

I completed a practical case from the GoPractice "Product Manager" program. The case covers how to identify the tasks and problems of a product's users.

The case author is Lyuba Vyaznikova (former Chief Product Officer at Bumble).

GoPractice mentor *Nikita Blinkov (Lead Product Manager, AI Infra/ML, at Open Innovation)* helped me with this case.

## Task

Conduct a JTBD study of Tinder users and prepare a presentation with the results. Answer the following questions:

- What tasks does the product solve for users?
- What alternatives does it have for these use cases?
- What is the added value of our product compared to alternatives for the main use cases?
- What is the added value of the alternatives compared to our product for the same use cases?

## Actions

- I prepared a plan with questions for the JTBD interviews by adapting a template from the course, after first getting familiar with the dating app market.
- I conducted 4 JTBD interviews according to the prepared plan: two women and two men. I transcribed the conversations and reviewed the interviews to identify and fix mistakes in my interviewing approach.
- I classified:
  - the reasons respondents started using the product;
  - the tasks they tried to solve with the product;
  - the problems they encountered while solving those tasks;
  - the motivation to return to the product for old or new tasks;
  - the alternative ways of solving the tasks.
- Based on the tasks and problems identified in the respondents' answers, I proposed user value hypotheses.

## Result

Based on the 4 interviews, I formulated 3 value hypotheses for several respondent tasks: time killer, finding a person who is close in spirit and goals, and finding a partner. For each hypothesis, I proposed a solution and metrics to evaluate its effectiveness. The hypotheses still need to be tested.
