> Case study. Completed as a practical assignment for the GoPractice "Product Manager" program. Product: Joom; the work is not affiliated with the company.

## Situation

I completed a practical case from the GoPractice "Product Manager" program. The case concludes the topic of implementing a project based on a validated solution.

The case author is Anna Lazutkina (former CPO of the Joom marketplace).

GoPractice mentor *Nikita Blinkov (Lead Product Manager, AI Infra/ML, at Open Innovation)* helped me with this case.

## Task

Implement regular sales functionality within an international B2C ecommerce marketplace. Specifically:

- Assess the project's potential in terms of its value for users and for the business;
- Analyze the results of the experimental launch;
- Formulate the optimal solution based on the experiment and analysis;
- Work out the business logic of the solution;
- Plan the project and assign tasks to the responsible teams.

## Actions

1. I prepared the project for defense at quarterly planning.
   - a. I formulated hypotheses about user value (buyers and sellers) and business value.
   - b. I identified the key risks behind the project's value hypotheses. I proposed ways to validate these risks within 3 weeks.
   - c. I made a preliminary conclusion about the project's potential based on the experiments. I assigned analysts tasks to extract useful insights for further work on the project.
   - d. I prepared a presentation with ideas for potential solutions. I prepared a description of the logic with mockups for the most promising one.
2. I worked out the business logic using the Business Model Canvas.
3. I assigned a designer to work jointly on the UX and product interaction.
4. I answered questions from development and design about the proposed solution.
5. I created a project roadmap for my team and related teams involved in the launch. Within the quarter, the project was to be implemented and launched with an A/B test using a holdout group.

## Result

### Personal

- I identified the reasons why several technical projects I had led at work failed.
- I learned to communicate my thoughts more concisely and to separate what matters from what doesn't in order to launch the project on time.
- At each stage of the work, I planned and documented the next steps.

### Project

1. Product work on the feature was completed. The full lifecycle of launching regular sales with a deadline constraint was completed.
2. A/B tests were run and completed with minimal time and resource costs, followed by a data-based evaluation of the launch.
3. OKRs for the project were set based on data.
4. The business logic was clearly communicated to the development and design teams.
