> Case study. Completed as a practical assignment for the GoPractice "Product Manager" program. Product: Tinder; the work is not affiliated with the company.

## Situation

I completed a practical case from the GoPractice "Product Manager" program. The case concludes the topic of how to find and validate potential solutions to user problems and needs of a product.

The case author is Lyuba Vyaznikova (former Chief Product Officer at Bumble).

GoPractice mentor *Nikita Blinkov (Lead Product Manager, AI Infra/ML, at Open Innovation)* helped me with this case.

## Task

Validate the idea of a new feature for Tinder. Specifically:

- Gather context about the project from managers and stakeholders;
- Identify the key hypotheses behind the feature;
- Test the riskiest hypotheses.

Determine the best way to solve the users' problem and develop the feature before implementation, or decide not to implement it and justify the reasons.

## Actions

- Based on the initial brief, I formulated hypotheses about the problem and the solution, as well as hypotheses about user value and business value. I prepared questions for managers to clarify the context: preliminary analytics, user and business problems, business goals, the reasons the task arose, and the desired metric values.
- After receiving additional context, I refined the user and business value hypotheses and defined the steps needed to confirm them.
- For each hypothesis, I described a research design. For the JTBD interviews, I prepared an interview plan with questions. I also drafted a plan with possible next steps depending on the research results.
- Based on the interview transcripts with users, I identified the main reasons users leave for other messengers. I also defined stop factors that would argue against continuing work on the project.
- I wrote a memo to the leadership with evidence for why the project should be stopped.

## Result

A plan to confirm the hypotheses was created.

- I practiced the product discovery algorithm:
  - Clarifying the context of the task to understand why it arose and what business goals it serves;
  - Formulating problem and solution hypotheses, as well as user and business value hypotheses;
  - Creating a plan to confirm the hypotheses, including research designs;
  - Testing the riskiest hypotheses;
  - Processing research results and identifying reasons to stop work on the task;
  - Writing a report with next steps for the manager.
- Hypotheses about user and business value were formulated based on the refined context.
- Research was proposed to confirm them, and a JTBD interview plan was finalized.
